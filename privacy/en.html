Bubbels privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Bubbels is a bubble shooter for children, made by Mark Westerweel (westerweel.work). In short: Bubbels collects no data about you or your child.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No account, no sign-in, no name or email address needed.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party software watching along.
  • The game itself sends nothing over the internet: no data to us and none to anyone else.

What stays on the device

The game remembers, on the device itself, how many stars each level got, whether sound is on or off and whether the full version was bought. That stays on the device and never reaches us. Delete the app (or clear the site data in your browser) and it is gone.

Buying the full version

Levels 11 to 30 can be bought once in the app. Payment goes entirely through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We see no name, no payment details and no account: the app only asks the store, on the device, whether the purchase exists. For that part, Google's or Apple's privacy policy applies. Buying and restoring sit behind a question for parents, so a child cannot buy anything by accident.

The website

The web version of Bubbels is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may keep technical data such as your IP address in its logs for security. We have no access to those; GitHub's privacy statement covers them. The app itself does not use GitHub.

Children

Bubbels is made for children. Because the game collects nothing, no children's data is processed either.

Questions

Email mark@westerweel.work. If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
