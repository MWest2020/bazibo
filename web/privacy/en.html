Bubbels privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 (save data and backup added)

Bubbels is an app of games for children (the first one is a bubble shooter), made by Mark Westerweel (westerweel.work). In short: Bubbels collects no data about you or your child.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No account, no sign-in, no name or email address needed.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party software watching along.
  • The app itself sends nothing over the internet: no data to us and none to anyone else.

Save data: what stays on the device

The app remembers, on the device itself, how many stars each level got, whether sound is on or off, the chosen language and whether the full version was bought. That never reaches us.

In the Android and iPhone app this save data is kept in the app's own settings storage, so it is part of the phone's own backup: Android backup to your Google account, or the iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, depending on how you set up your phone. That way the progress comes along to a new phone, without an account. Google or Apple handle that backup under your own account; we cannot see it. Delete the app (or clear the site data in your browser) and the data on the device is gone.

Buying the full version

Every game has a free part (in the bubble shooter: levels 1 to 10). The full version, which opens everything in every game, can be bought once in the app. Payment goes entirely through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We see no name, no payment details and no account: the app only asks the store, on the device, whether the purchase exists. For that part, Google's or Apple's privacy policy applies. Buying and restoring sit behind a question for parents, so a child cannot buy anything by accident.

The website

The web version of Bubbels is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may keep technical data such as your IP address in its logs for security. We have no access to those; GitHub's privacy statement covers them. The app itself does not use GitHub.

Children

Bubbels is made for children. Because the app collects nothing, no children's data is processed either.

Questions

Email mark@westerweel.work. If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
