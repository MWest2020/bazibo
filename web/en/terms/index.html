Who

Bazibo is made by Westerweel Work (Mark Westerweel). Contact: hallo@bazibo.nl. Website: https://bazibo.nl.

What Bazibo is

Bazibo is a set of games for children. Part of every game is free to play. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no accounts.

The full version

  • One in-app purchase, "Unlock everything", opens everything in every game. In the Netherlands it costs €1; in other countries Google Play or the App Store sets the price.
  • You pay once. The purchase works on every device signed in with the same Google or Apple account, also after reinstalling ("Restore purchase").
  • Buying is meant for parents, so a question for adults comes first.

Payment and refunds

Payment goes through Google Play or the App Store, under their terms. Refunds are requested there. Under EU consumer law, the right of withdrawal ends for digital content supplied right after your explicit request; the refund policy of Google Play or the App Store still applies. If you get stuck, mail hallo@bazibo.nl.
