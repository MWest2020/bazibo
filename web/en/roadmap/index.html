What is here and what is coming

For parents. Updated: 18 November 2026

Get it on Google Play App Store: coming soon

In Bazibo now

  • Bubble Shooter: aim and pop, 30 levels.
  • Dogudoku: a logic puzzle with puppies, from 4 by 4 to 8 by 8.
  • Picturedoku: sudoku with pictures; older children can switch to numbers.
  • Pairs: turn over cards and find the pairs, on your own, together or against a kind computer. The full version has more cards, and picture-book and shapes cards too.
  • Jigsaw: drag the pieces to their places, 9, 16 and 25 pieces (age 3, 4 and 5) with three pictures. The full version adds 30, 48 and 60 pieces and more pictures.
  • Four in a Row: together on one screen or against the computer.
  • Game of the Goose: our own board with the classic squares; your child can count the squares themselves. In the full version with four players and a winter and a sea board.
  • Checkers: puppy against kitten on an 8 by 8 board; in the full version also the international 10 by 10 board and a smarter computer.
  • Quartets: the Dutch card game, against the computer or together on one phone; the cards you can ask for are big buttons. The full version has four players and 6 or 8 sets.
  • Snake: counting, letters and colours with a cheerful snake.
  • Colouring: tap to colour, and mandalas for older children.
  • Drawing: free drawing with a finger; in the full version also mirroring into a mandala, and stamps.
  • Piano: a toy piano with coloured keys, two fingers at once too. Play along with Frère Jacques: the next key lights up, and Listen plays the song to you. The full version adds Twinkle Twinkle, Au clair de la lune and Ode to Joy. For ages 7-10 a two-octave piano with black keys: the whole Ode to Joy free, and in the full version the Wilhelmus, In the Hall of the Mountain King, the Turkish March and Für Elise. On an upright phone the keyboard slides along with the song.
  • Drums: a colourful drum kit with eight drums and cymbals, two fingers at once too. Play along with a rhythm: after the count-in the next drum lights up. At your own pace for ages 4-6, on the beat for ages 7-10. Rock is free; the full version adds a march, Frère Jacques, a pony gallop and disco.
  • Telling Time: set and read the clock, analogue and digital, from whole hours to the 24-hour clock.
  • Letters: find the same letter among look-alikes (a capital with its small letter too), the missing first or middle letter of a pictured word, and build a word.
  • Kangaroo Hops: adding and taking away as taught in Dutch schools. A kangaroo stands on a number line and your child picks the hops. Up to 10 is free; the full version goes up to 20 (back too, past 10) and up to 100 (hops of 10, counting by 2s and 5s).
  • Map Puzzle: topography. Piece together the continents, Europe or your own country (the Netherlands, the United States or the United Kingdom), and find where a state, country, capital or ocean is. The continents and every map's puzzle are free; the full version adds "where is it?", the capitals and the maps without borders.
  • Little Hedgehog: a hedgehog walks through a garden maze and picks up the alphabet, the letters of a word or numbers in order. No hurry and no losing.
  • Endless play in the full version: new puzzles, boards and rounds every time.
  • Move progress: take the stars, colouring pages and drawings from one phone or tablet to another, with QR codes and without the internet (Settings).

What is coming

In this order, without dates: something goes into the app only when it works well.

  1. Sums: number bonds, adding and taking away, up to 10, 20 and 100.
  2. Counting money: pay with euro coins in a little shop.
  3. Word pairs: pairs of words and pictures, in Dutch and English.

Whatever comes: no ads, no account, no trackers, and anyone with the full version gets the new games at no extra cost.
