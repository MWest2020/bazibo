What is here and what is coming

For parents. Updated: 9 November 2026

In Bazibo now

  • Bubble Shooter: aim and pop, 30 levels.
  • Dogudoku: a logic puzzle with puppies, from 4 by 4 to 8 by 8.
  • Picturedoku: sudoku with pictures; older children can switch to numbers.
  • Four in a Row: together on one screen or against the computer.
  • Snake: counting, letters and colours with a cheerful snake.
  • Colouring: tap to colour, and mandalas for older children.
  • Telling Time: set and read the clock, from whole hours to the 24-hour clock.
  • Endless play in the full version: new puzzles, boards and rounds every time.

What is coming

In this order, without dates: something goes into the app only when it works well.

  1. Move progress: take the stars from one phone or tablet to another, with a QR code and without the internet.
  2. Letters with a read-aloud voice: a voice says a letter or sound, your child taps the right one. The voice is made in advance and ships inside the app; nothing goes to the internet.
  3. Sums: number bonds, adding and taking away, up to 10, 20 and 100.
  4. Counting money: pay with euro coins in a little shop.
  5. Word memory: pairs of words and pictures, in Dutch and English.

Whatever comes: no ads, no account, no trackers, and anyone with the full version gets the new games at no extra cost.
