# Keukentje (Little Kitchen)

What goes into bread and other dishes, so children learn it (Mark, 25 Nov 2026), in
`web/games/keuken/` (ages 3-10; the dishes in `dishes.js`, the rounds and what a tap means in
`rules.js`, both pure and tested; every picture drawn in code in `art.js`; the screens in
`keuken.js`). The child cooks in a little kitchen: a dish with empty bowls, and the ingredients to
tap; when every bowl is full the dish is ready. Sixteen dishes Dutch children know and others know
too (bread, pancakes, a toastie, pizza, vegetable soup, apple pie, salad, yoghurt with fruit, bread
with sprinkles, poffertjes, roti, rice with vegetables, pasta with tomato sauce, stamppot, a
smoothie, a peanut butter sandwich), with the ingredients kept correct and simple: no amounts, no
brands, nothing about allergies and never "good" or "bad" food. Nothing is ever wrong: a tap that
does not fit bounces back with a short line ("Dat hoort bij de pizza!"), and after two the answer
glows. No timers, nothing spoken.

- 3-5 "Wat hoort erbij?": two or three bowls, six pictures; the others plainly from other dishes.
- 6-7: every ingredient of a bigger dish from eight pictures with look-alikes (sugar next to salt,
  yoghurt next to milk), and "which dish can you make with these?" (the dish under a cover).
- 8-10: where an ingredient comes from (milk from the cow, cheese from milk, yeast is a tiny fungus), the
  steps of a simple recipe in order, and the odd one out.
- Rounds of 6 with stars by mistakes. Free: 3-5; 6-7, 8-10 and random rounds in the full version.
- Saved under `games.keuken`: stars per round, random rounds per tier, the how-to cards seen and
  the last tier.
