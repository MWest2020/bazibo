## Description (4000)

Kids games with no ads and no tracking: 27 games for toddlers, preschoolers and children up to 10. Puzzles, board games, drawing, music and learning games for letters, maths, money and telling time. Works offline.

LEARNING GAMES
• Telling Time: set the clock to "half past two" or pick what time it is, from a daily rhythm to single minutes and the 24-hour clock.
• Letters: find the same letter among look-alikes, the first or a middle letter of a pictured word, and build a word.
• Little Hedgehog: a hedgehog in a garden maze picks up the alphabet, a word or numbers in order.
• Kangaroo Hops: adding and taking away by hops on a number line, as taught at school. Up to 10 free; up to 20 and 100 in the full version.
• Little Shop: pay the mouse behind the counter with euro coins, later with notes, cents and change too. Whole euros are free.
• Little Kitchen: the cook, a little bear in a chef's hat, asks what goes into bread, pancakes or pizza; later also where milk comes from. Ages 3-5 free.
• Map Puzzle: piece together the continents, Europe, the Netherlands, the US or the UK, and find places. Free: the continents, and every puzzle with borders.

PUZZLES
• Dogudoku: hide the puppies, one per row and column, never side by side. From 4 by 4 to coloured beds; 60 puzzles.
• Picturedoku: sudoku with pictures, from age 3 without numbers or reading, up to 9 by 9. 60 puzzles.
• Pairs: on your own, together or against the computer. 6 and 12 cards; full version: 16 and 20 cards and more pictures.
• Jigsaw: 9, 16 and 25 pieces and a magnifier on phones; full version: up to 60 pieces and more pictures.
• Hide and Seek: lift a stone or look behind a bush: where is the hedgehog? Full version: forest, beach and farm.
• Train Tracks: lay one railway through every square, past the stations in order, and the little train rides. Ages 3-5 free.

BOARD GAMES: TOGETHER OR AGAINST THE COMPUTER
• Four in a Row, also on a little board where three in a row wins. Hard in the full version.
• Checkers: puppy against kitten, or classic, on 8 by 8. Full version: a smarter computer and 10 by 10.
• Tigers and Goats (Bagh-Chal), an old game from Nepal: goats hem tigers in, tigers jump over goats. Full version: a smarter computer.
• Chess: all the real rules. Tap a piece to see where it may go. Full version: a smarter computer.
• Game of the Goose: our own board; your child counts every square. Full version: four players, winter and sea boards.
• Ludo: a 6 brings a pawn onto the board; land on someone to send them back. Also Pachisi, the original from India with six shells. Full version: four players, a smarter computer.
• Quartets: ask for the cards you are missing. 4 sets; full version: 6 and 8 sets and a 4th player.

CREATIVE
• Colouring: pick a colour, tap a part. 13 pictures from age 3, mandalas from age 7.
• Drawing: with a finger, every colour and an eraser. Full version: mirror mode, stamps and up to 100 drawings.
• Piano: play along, the next coloured key lights up. Frère Jacques, and two octaves for ages 7-10.
• Drums: eight drums and cymbals. Play along at your own pace or on the beat.
• Parrot: say something and the parrot says it back, as a mouse or a bear too. Your voice stays on the device.

ACTION
• Bubble Shooter: aim and let go, and three of the same colour pop. 30 levels.
• Snake: eat the numbers in order, the letters of a word, or fruit of the right colour. A mistake is never game over.

MADE FOR CHILDREN
• Every colour has its own symbol or pattern, so colour-blind children can play too.
• Big buttons, short texts, in English or Dutch.

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
• No adverts, no account and no trackers: the app collects no data. PEGI 3.
• Every game has a free part. Paying once opens everything, in every game, including later ones, plus endless new puzzles, mandalas and rounds. No subscription. Buying is only possible after a question for parents.
