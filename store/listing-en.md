## Description (4000)

Bazibo: twenty-four games for children aged 3 to 10, with no ads, and more will follow. The name comes from "bāzi", Persian for play.

PUZZLES
• Dogudoku: hide the puppies, one per row and column, never side by side. From 4 by 4 to coloured beds; 60 puzzles.
• Picturedoku: sudoku with pictures, from age 3 without numbers or reading, up to 9 by 9, with numbers too. 60 puzzles.
• Pairs: on your own, together or against the computer. 6 and 12 cards with fruit; full version: 16 and 20 cards, picture-book and shapes cards.
• Jigsaw: 9, 16 and 25 pieces with three pictures, and a magnifier on phones; full version: 30, 48 and 60 pieces and five more pictures.
• Hide and Seek: lift a stone or look behind a bush: where is the hedgehog? If not there, another little animal waves. Full version: forest, beach and farm.

BOARD GAMES: TOGETHER OR AGAINST THE COMPUTER
• Four in a Row, also on a little board where three in a row wins. Hard in the full version.
• Checkers: puppy against kitten, or classic, on 8 by 8. Full version: a smarter computer and 10 by 10.
• Tigers and Goats (Bagh-Chal), an old game from Nepal: goats hem tigers in, tigers jump over goats. Play either side; full version: a smarter computer.
• Chess: all the real rules. Tap a piece to see where it may go. Easy with a hint; full version: a smarter computer.
• Game of the Goose: our own board; your child counts by tapping every square. Against a computer goose; full version: four players, winter and sea boards.
• Ludo: a 6 brings a pawn onto the board; land on someone to send them back. Your child counts. Also Pachisi, the original from India with six shells. Full version: four players, a smarter computer.
• Quartets: ask for the cards you are missing. 4 sets; full version: 6 and 8 sets and a 4th player.

CREATIVE
• Colouring: pick a colour, tap a part. 13 pictures from age 3, mandalas from age 7.
• Drawing: with a finger, every colour and an eraser. Full version: mirror mode (every stroke a mandala), stamps and up to 100 drawings.
• Piano: play along, the next coloured key lights up. Ages 4-6: Frère Jacques; 7-10: two octaves and the Ode to Joy. More songs in the full version.
• Drums: eight drums and cymbals. Play along at your own pace (4-6) or on the beat (7-10). Rock is free; four more rhythms in the full version.
• Parrot: say something and the parrot says it back, as a mouse or a bear too; full version: backwards, echo, choir and balloon. Your voice stays on the device.

LEARNING
• Telling Time: set the clock to "half past two" or pick what time it is, from a daily rhythm to single minutes and the 24-hour clock.
• Letters: find the same letter among look-alikes, the first or a middle letter of a pictured word, and build a word.
• Little Hedgehog: a hedgehog in a garden maze picks up the alphabet, a word or numbers in order.
• Kangaroo Hops: adding and taking away by hops on a number line, as taught at school. Up to 10 free; up to 20 and 100 in the full version.
• Map Puzzle: piece together the continents, Europe, the Netherlands, the US or the UK, and find places. Free: the continents, and every puzzle with borders.

ACTION
• Bubble Shooter: aim and let go, and three of the same colour pop. 30 levels.
• Snake: eat the numbers in order, the letters of a word, or fruit of the right colour. 30 levels; a mistake is never game over.

MADE FOR CHILDREN
• Every colour has its own symbol or pattern, so colour-blind children can play too.
• Big buttons, short texts, in English or Dutch. Works without internet.

NO ADS, EVER
• No adverts, no account and no trackers: the app collects nothing.
• Every game has a free part. Paying once opens everything, in every game, including later ones, plus endless new puzzles, mandalas and rounds. No subscription. Buying is only possible after a question for parents.
