## Description (4000)

Bubbels is a bubble shooter for children, without ads. No videos in between, no buttons that jump to another app, no account. Just aim, shoot and pop!

HOW IT WORKS
• Drag to aim and let go to shoot.
• A dotted line shows where the bubble goes, also off the wall. The bubble always lands exactly where the line points.
• Three or more of the same colour pop, with a real pop sound.
• Bubbles that hang on nothing fall down.
• Level 1 shows how it works with an animated hand.

MADE FOR CHILDREN
• Glossy bubbles, each colour with its own symbol (circle, star, triangle, diamond, heart, square), so colour-blind children can play too.
• Big buttons, very little text. The game's text is in Dutch; young children play it by sight.
• Works without internet.

NO ADS, EVER
• No adverts, not even in the free version.
• No account and no trackers: the game collects nothing.
• Levels 1 to 10 are free. Levels 11 to 30 can be bought once, together, with no subscription. Buying is only possible after a question for parents.
